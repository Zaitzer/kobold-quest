How I approach a monitoring problem

The same five steps whether the data is a bearing, a pump or a conveyor.

  1. Start from the physics.What should a fault look like, and at which frequency? A bearing's geometry and shaft speed tell you where to look.
  2. Reduce the signal to a few meaningful features.Envelope spectrum, band energy, normalised so a gain change does not look like damage.
  3. Learn what healthy looks like, robustly.Median and MAD instead of mean and standard deviation, so one odd reading does not move the baseline.
  4. Test sequentially.CUSUM accumulates small deviations that a single-sample threshold would miss. Tune it against false alarms and detection delay.
  5. Report in the plant's language.A plot an operator can read, a lead time in hours, and an honest list of what the model cannot see.
