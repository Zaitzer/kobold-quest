    How I did it

    Source: the Our World in Data energy dataset (legacy release, CC BY 4.0), which takes its electricity figures from Ember's yearly electricity data. Six countries, 2000 to 2025, all in terawatt-hours (TWh).

    • Fossil is coal, gas and oil together. Bio and other is bioenergy plus other renewables.
    • Checked that the sources add up to total generation for every country and year, and that generation plus net imports equals demand. Both hold to within 0.01 TWh.
    • Iceland has no 2025 figures in this release, so its latest year is 2024.
    • The Norway trend line is ordinary least squares; r is the Pearson correlation over all 26 years. A correlation over 26 points shows a pattern, not a mechanism: the mechanism here is that water in the reservoirs decides how much Norway can produce.
