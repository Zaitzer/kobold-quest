# The story: the Hatching (v6, 19 Oct 2026)

This file is the story. Where `story-bible.md` or the game disagree with it, this file wins; the game still plays an
older opening and will be rebuilt to match (see "What the game needs" at the end).

**Cozy on top, a proper story underneath.** Small kobolds, buns, raptors, jokes. Underneath: **taking part in a
democracy**, never named (no votes, parties or elections in the text). It's Pip's story, told the same way every
time; the player plays the battles, the exploring and the party, and makes no story choices.

**The allegory, for us writers only**

| in the story | stands for |
|---|---|
| dragon eggs | parties / candidates |
| embers (one per kobold) | votes |
| the hatching | an election |
| the guardian dragon | the government |
| buns | what a society makes and shares |
| the guards | military might, and the loudest voices |
| Bib | indifference: "why bother?" (acedia, the real sloth) |
| a dragon hatched from too few embers | what you get when few take part |
| the seven companions | seven ways to waste or misuse your ember (the deadly sins) |
| the Dragon Smiler, loaded with everyone's embers | what a whole people can do together |

## The world

- **Kobaldia.** Kobolds are cold-blooded, so warmth is everything. **Ember-buns**, baked with volcano fire, stay warm
  for hours. The whole country makes them together: farmers, millers, miners, bakers, and riders on **raptors** who
  carry it all. No town can make a bun alone.
- **Hearthspire**, the capital in the middle of the map: marble streets, a castle, the **Great Nest** in the castle
  yard, and the **tower fire** every town's hearth was lit from.
- **The guardian's job** is keeping raiders (dwarves, elves, humans, after the buns) off the border. Every dragon does
  that, hence the stay-home excuse: "a dragon is a dragon, what difference does it make?" The difference is who the
  buns go to. (Always raiders, never "strangers".)
- **Why the hatching exists** (three layers):
  1. A dragon egg needs more warmth than any kobold has. It hatches only from many embers; every kobold has exactly one.
  2. A dragon becomes like whatever warmed it. Warmed by everyone, it looks after everyone; warmed by a few, it hatches
     half-cold and corrupt.
  3. Long ago a **dragon king** ruled, with kobolds as his hoard-keepers, until every town carried fire to his mountain
     on the same night. The hatching was made so no dragon would answer to only a few again. Nobody alive remembers;
     it's a nursery rhyme and some faded murals.
- **Where the eggs come from:** before a guardian grows old and goes to sleep, she lays a clutch of three. Each egg has
  a temperament (sprouty, sleepy, shiny): that sets *what kind* of guardian it will be. The warmth sets *what kind of
  heart*. No egg is evil; the shiny one only looks fancy because Smudge tied a ribbon on it.
- **The dawn rule:** at dawn the warmest egg hatches, however little warmth that is. Three embers are enough to hatch
  a dragon, and far too few to give it a warm heart.
- **The Dragon Smiler** was what the kobolds used on the dragon king. Afterwards they took it apart and gave every town
  one piece, so that nobody could ever use it alone: only all of them together. It doesn't destroy anything. It fires
  embers, and a corrupt dragon hit with the warmth of the whole country at once curls back into its egg, ready to be
  hatched properly.
- **The rhyme** (Mallow sings it while she bakes)
  > When a dragon hatches cold
  > and he hoards the buns and gold,
  > bring a piece from every town,
  > build the Smiler, bring him down.
  > Load it with each kobold's ember,
  > aim it true, and then remember:
  > warmed by few, they're mean and small;
  > warmed by all, a dragon serves us all.

## The cast

- **Pip** (hero). Small, grumpy, big stick. Two of Mallow's buns every morning; life is good. He cares, but he's easily
  talked out of it. Grumpy all game; what changes is what he does. The player picks his class.
- **Bib** (antagonist), Pip's best friend, on his bench in Hearthspire. "Buns are fine. Nothing ever changes. Why
  bother?" He once asked everyone to help fix the old mill and nobody came, because everything was fine. **He never
  learns.**
- **Old Mallow** (mentor), the baker. She hands out the embers on hatching day, sings the rhyme, hides buns in her hat,
  and keeps her grandmother's recipe book, which has the Smiler's instructions in the back.
- **Snatchclaw**, the guard captain, and his guards. They stand watch every night and think the hardest workers should
  get the most buns: all of them. He never cheats; he only needs people to stay home while he takes one small step at
  a time. "They didn't come when it was free. They won't come now."
- **Glitterbeak**, the dragon hatched from three embers: corrupt and greedy, guards only the hoard, grows with every
  stolen bun.
- **At the nest:** Bramble (sprouty: "work never hurt a kobold"), Mallow (sleepy: her oven is old), Glint (shiny: he
  got a sticker from **Smudge**, Snatchclaw's friendly cousin).

## Classes (the classic D&D twelve)

Fighter, barbarian, paladin, ranger, rogue, monk, cleric, druid, bard, wizard, sorcerer, warlock. Today's warrior
becomes fighter and mage becomes wizard (save keys stay); the level-8 promotions become subclasses. Built in two waves.

| class | kobold flavour | in battle |
|---|---|---|
| fighter | capital soldier | solid front line |
| barbarian | volcano-born | huge damage, stronger when hurt |
| paladin | a guard who kept her oath | protector, smites, a little healing |
| ranger | raptor scout | range, marks a target |
| rogue | sapper and pickpocket | traps, sneak attacks, slips past lines |
| monk | fort-builder | fast, many small hits, stuns |
| cleric | temple healer | healing and protection |
| druid | Mossback farmer | vines that root, plant cover |
| bard | town crier | songs that buff, rumours that confuse |
| wizard | scholar | big area spells, fragile |
| sorcerer | ember-blooded (kobolds have dragon blood) | raw, unstable fire |
| warlock | made a pact with something odd (a talking teapot) | curses, steady damage |

## The seven companions: seven ways to waste an ember

Each joins in a town, has a want of their own, and ends the game warming an egg for the right reason.

| sin | companion | class | joins | how it spoils their ember | how they change | their own want |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Envy** | Wizzle | sorcerer | Hearthspire | too young for an ember, envies everyone with one: "you HAD one and you KEPT it?!" | turns envy into wanting to take part; his first ember | to be taken seriously |
| **Lust** (infatuation) | Glint | bard | Hearthspire | falls for the shiniest promise every time (he warmed the shiny egg for a sticker) | learns to look past the ribbon | a shiny thing that's actually his |
| **Sloth** | Bork | monk ("Bork is meditating") | the capital gate | napped through the hatching | shows up | to never nap through anything again |
| **Greed** | Nugget | rogue | Pebblebrook | backs whatever fills his own pockets | learns his share depends on the whole chain | his grandad's duck-shaped nugget |
| **Pride** | Saffi | wizard | Sunhaven | "most kobolds are too dim to choose; only the clever should" | watches a "dim" farmer make the wisest choice in town | to finish one book |
| **Wrath** | Cinder | barbarian | Emberreach | wants to smash it all because it's imperfect | fix it, don't smash it | to make something nobody laughs at |
| **Gluttony** | Crumb | cleric | Saltspray | took the stay-home buns: "a full belly backs whoever filled it" | a bun today, or a bakery tomorrow | to cook for everyone, not just the guards |

Bork is the comic sloth. **Bib is the real one**: acedia, not caring at all. The worst sin in the story is the one that
never comes along.

## The towns

Five towns, each with: a job in the bun chain, a **keeper** who sets the town's own **challenge** (not a battle) and
gives its piece of the Smiler, Snatchclaw's step in the takeover (with a guard boss to beat), and a companion.

| town | job | keeper and challenge | Snatchclaw's step | piece | field move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pebblebrook** | the mill, the dam and the copper mine | Miller Ruddle: last spring the dam vote was lost by **one stone**, and the mine's shift circle hasn't met in nine years. Get the miners to the circle | **tolls**: Grubb sits on the mill, Snipe takes half the copper | **the tube**: the old mill axle | **raptors** (travel): the riders' stable is on the mill road |
| **Sunhaven** | honey and water | the head scribe: every choice is posted on two signs side by side, and nobody reads them. Find the drainer by reading the signs | **fear**: "beat our dragon and the elves walk in"; the guards drain the oasis "for the border" | **the sight**: a honey-glass lens | |
| **Emberreach** | the bakers' volcano ovens | the forge master: relight the ovens before Cinder smashes them | **seizing the ovens**. Glitterbeak sleeps in the volcano's heat and grows huge. Snatchclaw in person | **the igniter**: volcano flint | |
| **Frostwick** | ice cellars for the flour | the fort captain: the town that pushes back ("we build, we don't warm eggs"). Help build the fort; your plan loses; build the winner anyway; now they listen | **the draft**: the guards come to make kobolds into guards; the town refuses together | **the cooling fins**: the fort's oldest ice | |
| **Saltspray** | salt, the harbour and the island boats | the two harbour masters, both bad (one leaks, one overcharges): the fishers picked the leak they could patch. Help them patch it | **stay-home buns** for keeping out of it, and the island boats taken so the islands can't come | **the ember chamber**: an island ember-lantern | **boats** (water) |

Plus, from Hearthspire: **the instructions**, in the back of Mallow's recipe book.

## The story

### Act 1: Hearthspire

1. **Ordinary world.** Morning in Hearthspire: Mallow's buns, Bib on his bench, the guards yawning. The tutorial.
2. **The call.** Hatching day. Mallow hands Pip his ember ("keep it close, you chilly little lizard"). Three dragon
   eggs: **sprouty** (more buns, everybody helps at harvest), **sleepy** (everything stays as it is), **shiny** (on a
   pile of gold crumbs, with a ribbon: "the finest buns!"). Bramble, Mallow and Glint each back one; Glint warms the
   shiny egg for one of Smudge's stickers.
3. **Refusal.** Pip walks to the nest to warm the sprouty egg. Bib: "Why bother? A dragon is a dragon. You've got your
   buns." Pip pockets his ember and naps by the warm nest. Almost nobody comes. The three guards warm the shiny egg; with Glint's that makes four embers.
   At dawn, CRACK: **Glitterbeak**, cold-eyed, eats Mallow's whole tray. A notice on the bakery: EVERY BATCH TO THE GUARDS FIRST.
4. **The mentor.** "But I didn't choose any of this." Mallow: "No, dear. Somebody did." The one bun hidden in her hat
   (one of three in the game), the rhyme, and the instructions in her grandmother's book. Wizzle comes along, and Glint,
   whose ember helped hatch it.
5. **The threshold.** The capital gate, where the guards take a bun from everyone going out. Bork sits there, ashamed,
   and joins. Pip is going for buns, nothing more.

### Act 2: along the bun chain

6. **Tests, allies, enemies.** Pebblebrook, Sunhaven, Emberreach (see the towns). Speeches move nobody; a kobold's own
   trouble does. Snatchclaw's steps get bigger: tolls, then fear, then the ovens. Glitterbeak grows. Nobody knows what
   the Smiler is; every town guesses (a flute? a very long hat?).
7. **The approach.** Three pieces, and news on the road: Snatchclaw has announced **the last hatching**. Pip hurries
   home to warn the capital, the hardest town, because it's home. He calls everyone to the square. **Nobody comes**:
   exactly what happened to Bib. Snatchclaw invites him into the castle and shows him a throne going up in the yard. On the day, he'll put out the tower fire and seal the nest, and there will never be another.
8. **The ordeal.** "Nobody came, Pip. Nobody ever comes. Two buns every morning, for life. Just go home." Exactly
   what Pip had at the start, and after the empty square he believes it. **He takes it**, and sits on the bench with
   Bib: "See? Buns are fine." Wizzle goes on alone and the guards catch him. Cinder storms the gate and is thrown out.
   Mallow's old oven breaks.
9. **The reward.** Pip can't eat them: "They taste like the hoard." He gives them to Mallow; she gives him the last two
   she ever hid. He goes after Wizzle. His ember, cold in his pocket all game, glows: nothing magic, he's decided.

### Act 3: the road back

10. **The road back.** Frostwick, where the guards' draft camp holds Wizzle, and Saltspray. Snatchclaw sends the draft and the stay-home buns; Pip's own story is
    his argument: "I took the deal. Here's what it cost." The island boats come back, and the whole country sets out
    for Hearthspire.
11. **Resurrection.** The castle on the day of the last hatching, Snatchclaw reaching for the tower fire. Snatchclaw,
    then Glitterbeak. The Smiler clicks together: it's an **RPG-7**, and it's empty. The whole country loads it with
    their embers, Pip's last, and Smudge's. It fires the warmth of the whole country at once, and Glitterbeak curls back
    into his egg (cartoon rules: nobody is hurt).
12. **Return.** The new hatching: three eggs, Glitterbeak's shiny one back among them. The raiders come over the hill,
    see the whole country in the yard with embers glowing, and go home. Everyone warms an egg; the companions each for
    their own reason. A good guardian hatches by **one ember**, and every kobold in the yard is sure it was theirs.
    Buns are shared again. Bib is on his bench: "Why bother?" Pip walks past him, a bun in each hand.

## Shaping tools

The hero's journey is the whole story and the seven sins are the companions. The tools below work at other levels,
so they fit inside without competing.

### The companions: sin to virtue, along Dan Harmon's story circle

Each companion's side story runs through campfire chats and one small quest: **comfort, want, go, search, find, pay,
return, changed.** Where they end up is the virtue opposite their sin.

| companion | sin → virtue | comfort and want | search and find | the price | changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wizzle | envy → kindness | in Grandma's shadow, too young to count; wants to be taken seriously | glares at everyone with an ember; in Emberreach he meets kobolds who had embers and wasted them, and envy stops making sense | in the Ordeal he goes on alone, and the guards catch him | at the hatching, his first ember; he cheers everyone else's |
| Glint | lust → discernment | collects shiny things; wants one that's really his | falls for every shiny offer on the road; in Sunhaven the honey-glass lens teaches him to look *through* things, not at them | trades his whole sticker collection to get the lens back | looks at all three eggs properly; his own shiny thing is a plain pebble he chose himself |
| Bork | sloth → diligence | naps; wants never to nap through anything again | nods off at every big moment (running gag); in Frostwick he finds that work is nice when it's shared | stays awake all night guarding the fort against the draft | first one awake on hatching day, and he wakes the others |
| Nugget | greed → charity | nuggets; wants his grandad's duck-shaped nugget | haggles over every piece; finds the duck nugget in Glitterbeak's hoard in the castle | the nugget is copper, exactly what Mallow's broken oven needs | he gives it to the oven; the buns that come out are for everyone |
| Saffi | pride → humility | books; wants to finish one | lectures everyone; in Frostwick a "dim" builder's simple fort plan beats her clever one, and works | says so, out loud, in front of the whole fort | finishes her book (it was called *Why Only the Clever Should Choose*) and writes it a new ending |
| Cinder | wrath → patience | rage at anything imperfect; wants to make something nobody laughs at | wants to blow things up, and the Smiler tempts him; finds that fixing Mallow's oven is slow and good | in the Ordeal he storms the castle gate alone and nearly ruins everything | at the castle he holds the Smiler and waits until the whole country has loaded it |
| Crumb | gluttony → temperance | a full belly from stay-home buns; wants to cook for everyone, not just the guards | defends the deal; finds the islands cut off and hungry while she eats | gives up her bun ration | cooks the hatching feast from shared buns |

**The golden mean.** Cinder is too much caring (smash it all); Bib is too little (why bother). The virtue sits in
between: caring enough to fix things. That's where Pip ends up.

### The towns: four beats each (kishōtenketsu)

Introduction, development, **twist**, reconciliation. A small story per town, with no need for a mini hero's journey.

| town | introduction | development | twist | reconciliation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pebblebrook | the mill has stopped; Grubb's toll | the dam vote lost by one stone; getting the miners to the circle (Nugget haggles for everything) | **Grubb is a Pebblebrook kobold**; he went to the guards because nobody ever listened to him at the circle | the circle meets, Grubb included; the mill turns; the tube |
| Sunhaven | the oasis is draining; nobody reads the two signs | **Snatchclaw arrives with medals and the town cheers its protector** ("without our dragon the elves walk in"); Saffi lectures | read side by side, the signs show the water running to the guards' barracks, not the border | the town reads the signs together; the sight; Saffi joins |
| Emberreach | the ovens seized; Glitterbeak asleep in the volcano | Cinder wants to smash the ovens, the forge master wants to relight them | **the friendly cousin has been paying Cinder to smash them**, so the town would need the guards for bread | the ovens relit; the igniter; Cinder joins, furious at being used |
| Frostwick | the fort before the storm: "we build, we don't warm eggs" | Pip grumbles and gets a snowball; he helps; Saffi's clever plan against a builder's simple one | Pip's plan and Saffi's both lose, and the simple one works | the draft refused together; the fins; Bork stays awake all night |
| Saltspray | two bad harbour masters, the boats gone, stay-home buns | Crumb defends the deal; the fishers patch the leaky harbour | **the overcharging harbour master is in the guards' pay**: the stay-home buns come through him. The leaky one is at least honest | the boats come back; the ember chamber; Crumb joins |

### The villains: a False Hero and a Shapeshifter

- **Snatchclaw is the False Hero** (Propp): he claims the hero's job, "only my dragon keeps the raiders out", while
  doing the opposite. His big scene is Sunhaven, where people cheer him. Pip, at that point, half believes him too.
- **Smudge, the friendly cousin, is the Shapeshifter** (Vogler): hands out stickers at the nest, sells Glint shiny
  junk on the road, is unmasked in Emberreach paying Cinder, and in the finale switches sides: he loads his ember into
  the Smiler, because a sealed nest is sealed for him too.

### Small devices

- **Rule of three:** three eggs, three guards, three buns, three pieces before the midpoint, and Bib says "Why bother?"
  three times (at the start, in the Ordeal, at the end).
- **Chekhov's guns** (everything shown early pays off):

| set up | pays off |
|---|---|
| Pip's cold ember, carried all game | glows in the Reward; the last ember into the Smiler |
| Mallow's recipe book | the Smiler's instructions in the back |
| a faded mural in the castle hall of "the dragon smiler", which looks like a tube on a shoulder | it's an RPG-7 |
| Mallow's old oven that "likes things as they are" | breaks in the Ordeal; fixed with Nugget's nugget |
| the raiders mentioned once at the nest | come over the hill at the new hatching |
| the tutorial: Pip lights a lamp from the tower fire | Snatchclaw's bucket of water over that fire in the finale |

- **Ouroboros:** the story ends where it began, on Bib's bench.

## Review: theme, sense, need, feeling (19 Oct 2026)

Every beat and element checked against four questions: how it carries the theme, whether it makes sense inside
Kobaldia, whether the story needs it, and what the player should feel. Fixes from this review are already in the
story above.

### The beats

| beat | the theme | makes sense because | needed? | the player feels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Ordinary world | comfort is where indifference grows | kobolds love warmth and buns; life is good | yes: it's what's lost, and the ending returns to it | cozy, a little smug |
| 2 The call | the choice exists, and it's yours | the eggs are the old guardian's clutch; embers come from the tower fire via the bakers' ovens | yes | curious, amused (stickers, ribbons) |
| 3 Refusal | not choosing is a choice; the few decide | the dawn rule: the warmest egg hatches however cold | yes, the core of the message | "oh no", and a guilty "that was me" |
| 4 Mentor | responsibility: "somebody did" | Mallow is the one who hands out embers, so she knows who came | yes | the sting of that line, then a bun-shaped hope |
| 5 Threshold | the cost of the new rule, felt at the gate | the guards take their cut at every gate | yes, short | setting off |
| 6 Tests | taking part changes things, town by town; Snatchclaw takes one small step at a time | every town keeps a Smiler piece, by design | yes, it's most of the game | fun, then growing worry as Glitterbeak grows |
| 7 Approach | Pip becomes Bib: he calls a meeting and nobody comes | news of the last hatching sends him home | yes, it makes the Ordeal believable | dread; recognising Bib's story in Pip's |
| 8 Ordeal | indifference is comfortable, and it's a choice too | the deal is exactly the life Pip had | yes, the strongest beat | uncomfortable, sad, "I'd have taken it too" |
| 9 Reward | caring is a decision, not a feeling that arrives | nothing magic: the ember glows because he decided | yes | relief, resolve |
| 10 Road back | honesty about your own failure persuades better than speeches | Wizzle is in the draft camp; the boats are needed to bring the islands | yes | momentum, hope |
| 11 Resurrection | a whole people together is stronger than the loud few | the Smiler fires warmth, not harm; a dragon warmed by all curls back into its egg | yes | triumph, and a big laugh (the RPG-7) |
| 12 Return | a hatching with everyone; your one ember can be the one; some never learn | everyone came, so the good guardian hatches | yes | warm, proud, a little bittersweet (Bib) |

### The elements

| element | the theme | needed? | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buns | what a society makes and shares, felt in your stomach | yes | the reason the theme is cozy rather than preachy |
| Bib | indifference with a friend's face | yes | must stay likeable; the player should like him and be annoyed by him |
| Snatchclaw (False Hero) | the loud few, wrapped in protection | yes | his Sunhaven scene must be convincing; the player should half believe him |
| Smudge (Shapeshifter) | even those who gained switch sides when the door closes for everyone | yes, but small | three short scenes; don't let him grow |
| The guards | might and loud voices | yes | they're right that they work hard; keep that |
| Raiders | the guardian's real job, and Snatchclaw's fear | yes | seen twice: mentioned at the nest, met at the end |
| Seven companions | seven ways to waste an ember | yes | each lesson comes out in banter and their own quest, never in a speech |
| The Smiler | only everyone together can do it | yes | the RPG-7 joke keeps the most earnest moment funny |
| Three buns | scarcity you can feel in the bag | yes, gameplay | tie each one to a scene (hat, Reward x2) |
| Field moves, classes | none | gameplay only | fine: not everything must carry the theme |
| Mural, lamp, oven, book | none directly; setup and payoff | yes, cheap | they make the world feel planned |

### The emotional curve

Cozy and funny (1-2) → a guilty drop (3-4) → adventure and fun with rising worry (5-6) → dread (7) → the low point
(8) → resolve (9) → momentum (10) → triumph and laughter (11) → warmth with one bittersweet note (12).

### Holes found and fixed

1. **Where do the eggs come from, and why can three embers hatch one?** The old guardian's clutch; the dawn rule.
2. **Why is the Smiler in pieces, and what does it do?** Split up after the dragon king so no one could use it alone;
   it fires warmth, which is why a corrupt dragon curls back into its egg (and why it fits a cozy game).
3. **Why does Pip go home at the midpoint, and why would he take the deal after growing?** News of the last hatching;
   then he calls the capital together and nobody comes, which is Bib's story happening to him.
4. **Why does Glint come along?** His ember helped hatch Glitterbeak.
5. **Where is Wizzle rescued?** The draft camp at Frostwick.

## What the game needs (from the current build)

The game still plays an older opening (Burrowhome, the valley, sparks, bandits). To match this story:
- Hearthspire in the middle of the map; Burrowhome removed; the opening rewritten (Mallow hands out embers).
- Sparks become Smiler pieces; bandits become Snatchclaw's guards; "Mossback" monsters become **Nettlenose** scouts.
- The twelve classes (two waves) and the seven companions.
- Towns: Pebblebrook (now with the mine), Sunhaven, Emberreach, Frostwick, Saltspray. Copperhollow's content folds
  into Pebblebrook; Thistledown, Mossmere, Glimmerfen and Brindlecove become route stops or post-game.
- Field moves: raptors and boats.
- Fights: Grubb and Snipe (Pebblebrook), a guard boss per town, Snatchclaw (Emberreach, castle), Glitterbeak (castle).
