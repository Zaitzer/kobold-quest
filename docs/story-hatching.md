# The story: the Hatching (v5, 19 Oct 2026)

This file is the story. Where `story-bible.md` or the game disagree with it, this file wins; the game still plays an
older opening and will be rebuilt to match (see "What the game needs" at the end).

**Cozy on top, a proper story underneath.** Small kobolds, buns, raptors, jokes. Underneath: **taking part in a
democracy**, never named (no votes, parties or elections in the text). It's Pip's story, told the same way every
time; the player plays the battles, the exploring and the party, and makes no story choices.

**The allegory, for us writers only**

| in the story | stands for |
|---|---|
| dragon eggs | parties / candidates |
| embers (one per kobold) | votes |
| the hatching | an election |
| the guardian dragon | the government |
| buns | what a society makes and shares |
| the guards | military might, and the loudest voices |
| Bib | indifference: "why bother?" |
| a dragon hatched from too few embers | what you get when few take part |
| the Dragon Smiler, loaded with everyone's embers | what a whole people can do together |

## The world

- **Kobaldia.** Kobolds are cold-blooded, so warmth is everything. **Ember-buns**, baked with volcano fire, stay warm
  for hours; they're what keeps kobolds going.
- **The whole country makes buns together.** Mossbacks farm, millers grind, bakers use the volcano, riders on
  **raptors** carry it all, and every other town adds one thing. No town can make a bun alone.
- **Hearthspire**, the capital in the middle of the map: marble streets, a castle, the **Great Nest** in the castle
  yard, and the **tower fire** that every town's hearth was first lit from.
- **The guardian's job** is keeping raiders from outside (dwarves, elves, humans, who come for the buns) away from the
  border. Every dragon does that, which is why the stay-home excuse is "a dragon is a dragon, what difference does it
  make?" The difference is what happens inside: who the buns go to. (Raiders, never "strangers": this must not read as
  one side of the immigration debate.)
- **Three layers of why the hatching exists:**
  1. A dragon egg needs more warmth than any kobold has, so it only hatches from many embers. Every kobold has exactly one.
  2. A dragon becomes like whatever warmed it. Warmed by everyone, it looks after everyone; warmed by a few greedy
     kobolds, it hatches half-cold and corrupt.
  3. Long ago the **dragon king** ruled, with kobolds as his hoard-keepers. One night every town carried fire to his
     mountain together and he never came back; Hearthspire's tower fire is that night's fire. The hatching was made so
     no dragon would ever again answer to only a few. Nobody alive remembers; he's a nursery rhyme now.
- **The Elder's rhyme**
  > When a dragon hatches cold
  > and he hoards the buns and gold,
  > bring a piece from every town,
  > build the Smiler, bring him down.
  > Load it with each kobold's ember,
  > aim it true, and then remember:
  > warmed by few, they're mean and small;
  > warmed by all, a dragon serves us all.

## The cast

**Pip** (hero). Small, grumpy, big stick. Two of Mallow's buns every morning and life is good. He does care, but he's
easily talked out of it. He stays grumpy all game; what changes is what he does.

**Bib** (antagonist), Pip's best friend, a villager in Hearthspire. Indifference with a face: "Buns are fine. Nothing
ever changes. Why bother?" Why: he once asked the whole city to help fix the old mill, and nobody came; and nobody came
because everything was fine. **He never learns.** At the end he's on the same bench saying the same thing, and Pip
walks past him.

**Snatchclaw** (villain), captain of the guards. His family were the dragon king's hoard-masters, and he wants that
power back. He never cheats; he only needs people to stay home while he takes one small step at a time. "They didn't
come when it was free. They won't come now."

**The three guards** (his first followers). They stand watch every night and believe the hardest workers deserve the
most buns: all of them, ideally. They aren't wrong that they work hard. Only a guardian ever made them share.

**Glitterbeak**, the guardian hatched from three embers: corrupt, greedy, guards only the hoard. He grows with every
stolen bun.

**Old Mallow** (mentor), the baker. Gives Pip her hidden buns and the line that starts it all: "No, dear. Somebody did."
**Elder Mumble** keeps the embers and the rhyme, and eats pebbles while he thinks. **Witch Yarrow**, in the woods
outside the capital, remembers her grandmother's stories of the dragon king (the history, for those who look).

## The story

### Act 1: Hearthspire

**1. Ordinary world.** Morning in Hearthspire. Mallow's buns, Bib on his bench, guards yawning at the gate. The tutorial.

**2. The call.** Hatching day. Elder Mumble gives Pip his ember in a clay cup ("keep it close, you chilly little
lizard"). Three dragon eggs in the Great Nest:
- **sprouty** (green, a leaf on top): ploughs new fields; more buns for everybody, and everybody helps at harvest.
- **sleepy**: keeps everything just as it is.
- **shiny** (on a pile of gold crumbs, with a ribbon): "the finest buns!"

The crowd at the nest, one way of thinking each: Bramble (sprouty: "work never hurt a kobold"), Old Mallow (sleepy: her
oven is old), Glint (shiny: he got a sticker), Grumble ("somebody else always decides"), Sorrel (asked her cows), Dottie
(hammock: "it's fine like it is"), and a friendly kobold from the hills handing out stickers (Snatchclaw's cousin).

**3. Refusal.** Pip walks to the nest meaning to warm the sprouty egg. Bib is on the way. "Why bother? A dragon is a
dragon. You've got your buns." Pip puts the ember back in his pocket and naps by the warm nest. Almost nobody comes.
Only the three guards each warm the shiny egg. At dawn: CRACK. **Glitterbeak** hatches, cold-eyed and glittering, eats
Mallow's whole tray, and Snatchclaw nails a notice to the bakery: EVERY BATCH TO THE GUARDS' HOARD FIRST.

**4. The mentor.** No buns. "But I didn't choose any of this." Mallow: "No, dear. Somebody did." She gives Pip the one
bun she hid in her hat (one of three in the whole game). The Elder tells the rhyme: a piece from every town, the Dragon
Smiler, and a new hatching with everyone. Pip: "Sounds like a job for somebody else." "Mm. Somebody else is a very busy
kobold." Wizzle, too young for an ember: "You HAD one and you KEPT it?!"

**5. The threshold.** The capital gate, where the guards now take a bun from everyone going out. Bork is sitting there,
ashamed: he napped through the hatching. He comes along to make up for it. Pip is going for buns, nothing more.

### Act 2: along the bun chain

Each town has its own job in the chain, its own way of deciding things (with something broken in it), one step of
Snatchclaw's takeover, a recruit, and a piece of the Smiler. Nobody knows what the Smiler is; every town guesses
(a flute? a bun pipe? a very long hat?).

**6. Tests, allies, enemies.**

| town | job | way of deciding, and what's broken | Snatchclaw's step | what happens | Smiler piece |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pebblebrook** | the mill | the dam stone vote: every kobold brings one stone. Last spring it was lost by **one stone**; Turnip's family of five stayed home | **tolls**: Grubb sits on the mill, one shiny thing a sack | Pip clears Grubb's lookouts and Grubb. Fennel, whose sleepy egg lost, is cheerful anyway | **the tube**: the old mill axle, hollow, stuck since the dam vote |
| **Copperhollow** | copper ovens and pans | the shift circle round the hearth; nobody has come in nine years, so Ruddle decides alone | **taking half**: Snipe takes half the copper, because nobody comes to say no | four miners talked round with their own reasons; Pip hears his own excuses in theirs ("...Huh") | **the trigger**: cast in copper by the first circle in nine years |
| **Thistledown** | the raptor riders' crossroads and market | two guild councils, bakers and millers, who never agree | **divide and rule**: the guards favour one guild, and start paying **stay-home buns** to anyone who keeps out of it | half the town took the buns; they're hungry, not wrong. The guilds mend the bridge together. **Tikka**, fight 1: "I'll do it alone, faster" | **the strap**: one half from each guild; useless apart |
| **Sunhaven** | honey and water for the dough | the oasis scribes post every choice on two signs side by side; nobody reads them any more | **fear**: "Beat our dragon and the elves walk in." Guards drain the oasis "for the border" | Saffi has read everything twice and can't choose; Pip makes her read two signs and pick. The raptor riders join the cause | **the sight**: a honey-glass lens the scribes polish |
| **Emberreach** | the bakers' volcano ovens | the forge vote, by whoever shouts loudest | **seizing the ovens**: the guards take the bread itself. Glitterbeak sleeps in the volcano's heat and grows huge | Cinder wants to smash every oven so the guards get nothing. Snatchclaw cheers him on; Pip sees who's clapping. Snatchclaw in person for the first time | **the igniter**: volcano flint from the oldest oven |

**7. The approach.** Five pieces. Pip goes home to win over the capital: the hardest town, because it's home. Bib's
bench. The guards everywhere. Snatchclaw invites Pip into the castle, and shows him a throne going up in the yard and a
date for **"the last hatching"**: he'll put out the tower fire and seal the nest, and there will never be another
hatching. Lord Snatchclaw and his dragon, for good.

**8. The ordeal.** "Two buns every morning, Pip. For life. Just go home." Exactly what Pip had at the start. **He takes
it.** He sits on the bench with Bib: "See? Buns are fine." Wizzle goes on alone. The towns' pieces sit in Pip's bag.
Mallow's oven goes dark.

**9. The reward.** Pip can't eat them: "They taste like the hoard." He gives his two buns to Mallow, and she gives him
the last two she ever hid (buns two and three of three). He goes after Wizzle. His cold ember, carried all game, glows
warm in his pocket: nothing magic, he's just decided.

### Act 3: the road back

**10. The road back.** Snatchclaw sends Glitterbeak out to bring the towns to heel before the last hatching. Pip is no
longer asking for help; he's bringing the whole country to Hearthspire, and his own story is his argument: "I took the
deal. Here's what it cost."

| town | job | way of deciding, and what's broken | Snatchclaw's step | what happens | Smiler piece |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Frostwick** | ice cellars that keep the flour dry | the fort meeting: everyone argues, everyone builds | **conscription**: the guards come to draft kobolds into the guard | **the town that pushes back**: "Warm an egg and walk away? Pah. We build." Pip turns up to watch and gets a snowball: you didn't build, you don't get a say. He helps; his fort plan loses; he builds the winner anyway, and now they listen. They refuse the draft together. Pell. **Tikka**, fight 2 | **the cooling fins**: carved from the fort's oldest ice |
| **Glimmerfen** | yeast from the marsh | gossip at the well | **rumours**: the guards spread "the Smiler is a lie" and "Pip is an elf spy" | Pip passes on a rumour himself and it's wrong; he goes back to everyone he told. With Mist he traces the guards' rumours to their source | **the grip**: marsh cork |
| **Saltspray** | salt | two harbour masters, both bad: one leaks, one overcharges; the fishers picked the leak they could patch | **the real threat**: human raiders' sails offshore, and the guards say only Glitterbeak can stop them | Pip helps the patched harbour stand; the raiders turn back from a harbour full of kobolds. Skipper Gull gets her boat back | **the rocket**: a smuggler's old buoy, exactly the right shape |
| **Mossmere** | the Mossback farms, the wheat itself | nobody decides; Granny Fern has kept the moss walls up alone for fifty years | **comfort**: Mossmere gets the most stay-home buns of anywhere, and it's fine | Fern retires, the walls start to fail where the old and the small live, and the town finally learns what she did. Thimble takes it on | **the instructions**: in Fern's notebook. Her grandmother built the last Smiler |
| **Brindlecove** | the island bridges and boats | the island moot, which nobody from the islands can reach the capital to join | **blocking the way**: the guards take the bridge-boats, so the islands can't come to any hatching | Pip and the riders bring the boats back. Wren, who has never seen the capital | **the ember chamber**: an island ember-lantern, where the embers go |

**The great ride.** The night before the last hatching, the raptor riders carry the whole country to Hearthspire:
farmers, millers, bakers, miners, islanders. Glitterbeak hunts the convoy across the map. **Tikka**, fight 3, at the
capital gate: she's still trying to do it alone; she loses, and rides in at the back.

**11. Resurrection.** Hearthspire, the day of the last hatching. Snatchclaw is on his throne with a bucket of water
over the tower fire. The castle fight: the guards, then Snatchclaw. Then Glitterbeak. The Smiler is assembled, and
when the last piece clicks in, it turns out to be... an **RPG-7**. It's empty. One by one, the whole country loads it
with their embers, Pip's last. It fires. Glitterbeak is blasted back into his egg (cartoon rules: nobody is hurt).

**The night without a guardian.** No dragon on the border until dawn, and the raiders know it. They come over the hill
and find the whole country standing on the walls, every ember glowing. They go home.

**The new hatching.** Three eggs again: sprouty, sleepy, and Glitterbeak's shiny egg, back on the nest. Everyone comes.
Pip warms the sprouty egg. It hatches by **one ember**, and every kobold in the yard is sure it was theirs. A good
guardian, warm and a bit clumsy, warmed by everyone.

**12. Return with the elixir.** Buns are shared again, and Mallow's oven gets fixed (the sprouty dragon helps). A
fourth bowl stands by the nest from now on: "none of these, try again", and it counts; Tikka puts her ember in it at
the next hatching, and smiles for once. The Elder asks Pip to help choose the eggs next time. Bib is on his bench:
"Why bother?" Pip walks past him, a bun in each hand.

## After the story

Post-game: Frostpeak, where the Old Guardian sleeps and remembers the dragon king; Snatchclaw's three guards, now
working at the bakery ("hardest workers get the most buns": they're very good at it); rare recruits; harder bounties.

## What the game needs (from the current build)

The game still plays the v2/v3 opening (Burrowhome, the valley, sparks, bandits). To match this story:
- Hearthspire in the middle of the map, with the Great Nest and tower; Burrowhome removed; the opening re-scripted.
- Sparks become Smiler pieces (flag `seals` → pieces; the quest log and markers follow).
- Bandits become Snatchclaw's guards; the "Mossback" monsters become **Nettlenose** scouts and slingers.
- Two new recruits (Thimble, Wren); Bork joins at the capital gate.
- New fights: Snatchclaw (Emberreach, castle), Glitterbeak (convoy, castle), Tikka (three times).
- Pebblebrook and Copperhollow keep their steps; their text moves to the new story.
