# Kobaldia: scope and milestones (draft)

*Step 1.8 of the roadmap, the last of pre-production.*

## Version 1.0 (for friends)

**In:**
- The whole story: 8 sparks across 10 towns, the villain thread, Tikka's three battles, the Old Guardian, the second hatching.
- 15 routes with their own monsters and materials, plus Frostpeak, the sewers, the crater and the hideout.
- 8 recruitable kobolds plus Bork, Wizzle and Tikka; classes, talents, promotions; gear, sets, crafting.
- Fountains with respawn and fast travel; the shared world with co-op, level sync, shared boss credit, duels, gifts, the
  market, chat, yips, letters to Quill.
- Joakim's drawings for every character and monster met in the story (tiles optional).
- Music and sounds for every kind of place and every big moment.

**Out (later):**
- Post-game beyond a small taste (Frostpeak trials, Snatchclaw's stash).
- Walking frames, portraits, drawn houses.
- Async notes on signs and gifts at fountains (nice, not needed).
- Everything in "Before going public" in the tech plan.
