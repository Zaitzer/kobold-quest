# Kobold Quest: the slice's dialogue (22 Oct 2026)

*Roadmap step 2.3. Every spoken line in the vertical slice: act 1 in Hearthspire, the capital gate, R1, Pebblebrook and its
mine, and the slice's campfire chats. Written from `story-hatching.md` (v6), placed with `town-kit.md` and the maps in
`docs/maps/` (the places named here are the maps' marks). Nothing in the story is changed.*

## 0. How to read this, and the rules the lines follow

- `**Name:** line` is one spoken line (one text box on the phone). *Italics* are what the player sees happen; they are not
  lines. A box holds at most about 90 characters, so no line here is longer than that.
- Each scene says **where** it is, **when** it plays (the flag it needs) and **what it sets**. The flags are new v6 names
  (§7); the engine's old ones (`moot`, `pb`, `ch`) go at the progress reset.
- **The text never says vote, party, election or democracy.** Pebblebrook's circle drops **stones in a bag**; the hatching
  is warming eggs. The allegory stays underneath.
- **Nobody gives a speech.** Every miner comes because their own small trouble is fixed, never because someone talks well.
- **Pip is grumpy all game.** In act 1 he's also easily talked out of things; what changes later is what he does, not his tone.
- **Voices:** Mallow is warm and dry ("dear"); Bib is kind and never there when it counts; Snatchclaw is tired, polite and
  never cheats; Smudge is a salesman; Wizzle shouts in capitals; Glint loves shiny things; Bork talks about himself as
  Bork; Nugget prices everything; Glitterbeak says MINE and is always cold.
- **The four review questions** (`story-hatching.md`) were asked of every line: does it carry the theme, does it make sense
  in Kobaldia, does the story need it, what should the player feel. A line that answered "no" to the third was cut.
- **Rule of three, kept:** Bib's "Why bother?" is said at the nest here, and nowhere else in the slice (the other two are the
  Ordeal and the end). Mallow's first of three buns is the one in her hat.

## 1. Act 1: Hearthspire

### 1.1 Morning (the bakery) · new game · sets `act 1`

*Morning. The bakery smells of volcano fire. Mallow slides two buns across the counter.*

**Mallow:** Two buns, Pip. Same as every morning.
**Pip:** Same as every morning. Good. I like things the same.
**Mallow:** So does my oven. It's older than me and twice as stubborn.
**Mallow:** Before you eat those: the cellar's got rats again. Take your stick.
**Pip:** It's a very good stick.
**Mallow:** It's a stick, dear.

### 1.2 The cellar (the bakery cellar) · `act 1` · tutorial fight: rats

*Pip goes down the hatch. Three rats are in the flour sacks. Moving and attacking are taught here.*

**Pip:** Out. This flour is for buns, and the buns are for me.
*After the fight Pip notices the old door in the cellar wall, boarded over.*
**Mallow:** *(from the hatch)* That's the flour tunnel. The carts used to come in under my bakery, before the roads.
**Pip:** Why doesn't anyone use it?
**Mallow:** It's full of rats.
**Pip:** It WAS full of rats.
**Mallow:** Then it's a very good stick.
**Mallow:** Now, one more job. Light my lamp at the tower fire, would you?
**Mallow:** Tomorrow is hatching day. The embers come out of my oven.
**Mallow:** And my oven wants a proper flame.

*Sets `act 2`.*

### 1.3 The square (Bib's bench) · `act 2`

**Bib:** Pip! Morning. Sit down, the bench is warm.
**Pip:** Can't. Errand. Lamp. Tower.
**Bib:** Hatching day tomorrow. Three eggs in the Nest. Everyone's talking about it.
**Pip:** Are you going?
**Bib:** Maybe. If I'm up. Here, have a bun. I had three.
**Pip:** You're the best friend a kobold ever had.
**Bib:** I know. I once asked the whole town to fix the old mill with me. Nobody came.
**Bib:** It was fine. Everything's fine.

*Bib's bench can be visited at any time in act 1; after this scene he says one of these (in turn):*

**Bib:** Buns are fine, Pip. Buns are always fine.
**Bib:** I'd help, but I'm sitting. You can't help AND sit. I've tried.
**Bib:** The fountain's nice today. It was nice yesterday too.

### 1.4 The castle yard (the drill) · `act 2` · tutorial fight: the recruits; **the class is picked here**

*The castle gate. Two guards, yawning.*

**Guard:** Halt. Who goes there? *(squints)* Oh. It's the bun kid.
**Guard:** Go on, then. Mind the drill.
*In the yard, a sergeant is shouting at recruits. Snatchclaw watches from the wall.*
**Sergeant:** YOU. Small one. Pick up something and show me what you are.
*The class picker opens: fighter, barbarian, paladin, ranger, rogue, monk, cleric, druid, bard, wizard, sorcerer, warlock
(wave 1 offers the six that are built). Then a short drill against two recruits teaches skills and mana.*
**Sergeant:** Not bad. For a kobold who eats that many buns.
**Snatchclaw:** *(from the wall)* Up early, bun boy? Good. Somebody should be.
**Pip:** Who's that?
**Guard:** The captain. Twenty winters on that wall. Never seen him sleep.

### 1.5 The castle hall and the tower · `act 2`

*The hall, on the way to the tower stairs. A faded mural: kobolds carrying fire up a mountain, and at the front a kobold
with a long tube on its shoulder. Underneath: THE DRAGON SMILER.*

**Pip:** Doesn't look like it smiles. Looks like a pipe.
*The tower fire at the top. Pip holds out the lamp; the flame jumps into it.*
**Tower guard:** Every hearth in Kobaldia was lit from that fire. Your baker's oven too.
**Tower guard:** Don't drop it.
**Pip:** I never drop things. *(He nearly drops it.)*

*Back at the bakery, Mallow lights the oven from the lamp. Sets `act 3`. Night falls; Pip sleeps.*

### 1.6 Hatching day (the bakery, then the square) · `act 3`

*Morning. Mallow lifts glowing embers out of the oven with tongs, one into each clay cup.*

**Mallow:** Here, Pip. Your ember. Keep it close, you chilly little lizard.
**Pip:** It's warm.
**Mallow:** That's the idea. Take it to the Nest and warm whichever egg you think best.
**Pip:** What if they're all bad?
**Mallow:** Then pick the least bad, dear. That's most of life.
*In the square only a handful of kobolds hold clay cups. Most are still in bed.*

### 1.7 The Great Nest (the castle yard) · `act 3`

*Three big eggs in the straw: a green one with little shoots, a grey one that seems to yawn, and a gold one on a pile of
gold crumbs with a ribbon on it.*

**Bramble:** That's the sprouty one. More buns, and everybody helps at harvest.
**Bramble:** Work never hurt a kobold.
**Mallow:** I'm warming the sleepy one. My oven's old. A sleepy dragon keeps things as they are.
**Glint:** Look at it SHINE. It's got a RIBBON.
**Smudge:** The finest buns, friend! A sticker for everyone who warms the shiny one. Sticker?
**Pip:** No.
**Smudge:** Suit yourself. *(to Glint)* Sticker?
**Glint:** *(already holding out his ember)* Yes please.
**Bramble:** Any of them will keep the raiders off the border.
**Bramble:** Dwarves, elves, humans. They all want our buns.
**Bramble:** The difference is who gets the buns afterwards.
*The player can look at each egg (one line each):*
**Pip:** *(sprouty)* It's growing a leaf. Show-off.
**Pip:** *(sleepy)* It's asleep. I respect that.
**Pip:** *(shiny)* It's wearing a ribbon. Eggs shouldn't wear things.

*Sets `act 4`.*

### 1.8 Why bother? (the Nest) · `act 4` · the refusal

*Pip walks to the sprouty egg, cup in hand. Bib wanders up behind him.*

**Bib:** Pip. Why bother? A dragon's a dragon. You've got your buns.
**Bib:** Here. Have mine too.
**Pip:** *(looks at the bun, then the egg, then the bun)* ...It's still warm.
*Pip pockets his ember, sits down in the warm straw and eats Bib's bun. He falls asleep.*
*Night. Almost nobody comes. Three guards walk in and hold their embers to the shiny egg.*
**Guard:** Captain says the shiny one.
**Guard:** Captain says.
*Glint is asleep next to the shiny egg, his sticker on his nose. The gold egg glows.*
*Dawn. CRACK.*
**Glitterbeak:** ...Cold. COLD. Why is everything so COLD?
*He sees Mallow's tray of morning buns, steaming. He eats the whole tray.*
**Glitterbeak:** Warm. MINE.
*He flies to the castle roof. Pip wakes up with straw in his ear.*
**Pip:** What did I miss?

*Sets `act 5`. A notice goes up on the bakery: EVERY BATCH TO THE GUARDS FIRST.*

### 1.9 Somebody did (the bakery) · `act 5` · the mentor

**Pip:** Every batch to the guards? But I didn't choose any of this.
**Mallow:** No, dear. Somebody did.
*Silence. Mallow kneads an empty bowl. Then she takes off her hat; there's a bun in it.*
**Mallow:** One left. I hid it in my hat. Don't tell the dragon.
*Mallow's bun (1 of 3) goes in the bag.*
**Mallow:** *(singing, quietly)* When a dragon hatches cold, and he hoards the buns and gold...
**Pip:** I know that one. It's for babies.
**Mallow:** It's for everybody. *(She takes down a fat recipe book.)* My grandmother's.
**Mallow:** Recipes in the front. In the back, instructions for a thing called the Smiler.
**Mallow:** A piece in every town. Bring them together and you can fix a dragon that hatched wrong.
**Pip:** So somebody should go and get them.
**Mallow:** Somebody should.
**Pip:** ...I'm going to Pebblebrook for flour. For buns. That's all.
**Mallow:** Of course you are, dear. Take the book.
*Wizzle bursts in.*
**Wizzle:** I'm COMING. I'm too young for an ember but I am NOT too young for this.
**Glint:** *(in the doorway, sticker still on)* I warmed the shiny one. For a sticker. ...I'll come too.
**Pip:** Fine. Nobody talk to me before lunch.

*Wizzle and Glint join. Sets `act 6`. The inn's first two campfire chats unlock (§6).*

## 3. Pebblebrook

### 3.1 Arrival (the mill road, Grubb's toll) · `act 7` · sets `pb 1`

**Grubb:** HALT. Toll. One bun.

### 3.2 Ruddle (the mill) · `pb 1` · sets `pb 2` (the challenge opens)

**Ruddle:** You're from the capital? Come for flour? Ha. Have a look at the wheel.
**Pip:** It's not turning.
**Ruddle:** The dam's shut. The brook goes round my mill instead of through it.
**Pip:** So open the dam.
**Ruddle:** Not alone, I can't. The dam is the whole town's. The shift circle decides it.
**Ruddle:** Everyone in the circle drops a stone in the bag. White, open it. Black, leave it.
**Ruddle:** Last spring the bag said leave it. By one stone.
**Pip:** So do it again.
**Ruddle:** With what circle? The miners haven't sat in it in nine years.
**Ruddle:** I've been counting. On the stone.
**Ruddle:** Get the whole shift to the circle and we'll try again. Five of them.
**Ruddle:** Well. Four who might come. And one who never does.
**Pip:** Why me?
**Ruddle:** You're the only one in town who hasn't said "why bother" yet.
**Pip:** *(who nearly did)* ...Fine. Who are they?
**Ruddle:** Ore, down the mine. Tansy, out by the pond.
**Ruddle:** Flint and Grit, who aren't speaking. And Old Marl.

*The quest log lists the five miners as five lines (one per step) with a hint each, one tap away.*

**Ruddle** (if asked about the Smiler)**:** A smiler? Never heard of one. A flute, maybe. For a very big kobold.
**Ruddle:** Old things? The only old thing here is my axle. Stuck since my grandad's day.

## 5. The ember stays cold

Pip carries his ember all game. In the slice he never mentions it; the player sees it in the bag as **"Pip's ember (cold)"**,
and once, when he holds up the tube at the mill (§3.12), Wizzle looks at his pocket and says nothing. That's the only hint.
