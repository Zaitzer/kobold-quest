# Kobold Quest: the companions, beat by beat (22 Oct 2026)

*Part of the design document (`design.md`). The arcs are the story's (`story-hatching.md`: the seven sins, the story circle
**comfort, want, go, search, find, pay, return, changed**); this file places each step in the game: which campfire chat, which
scene, which quest step, and what it does to the kobold's morale. Nothing in the story is changed.*

## 1. Rules for every companion

- **Lessons come out in banter and their own quest, never in a speech** (the story's rule).
- **Campfire chats** are short (6–12 lines), at an inn, when the companions in it are in the pack. **Four or five per companion,
  about 30 in all**, each unlocked by a story step, never by grinding. A chat between two companions counts for both.
- **Morale** (`balance.md` §3): each chat a kobold is in gives **+1 to its bond**, and finishing its quest gives **+1** more, kept
  for the rest of the game. A companion who has heard all its chats and done its quest has about **+5 bond**, so morale about 5
  before streaks: a chance of +1 AP of about 45% a turn. Getting to know your pack is literally worth something in a fight. The foe
  curve expects this bond (`balance.md` §3, P24), so the chats keep a pack on the curve rather than above it; skipping them makes it weaker.
- **One small quest each, never a battle** (the story's rule; fights may happen on the way).
- **Pinned absences:** Wizzle is away from the Ordeal to Frostwick; Cinder from the Ordeal until his second cellar visit. The
  roster says why ("Wizzle: caught by the guards").
- **Class in play:** each companion's class fits the arc, and a chat after their quest unlocks a small talent card for them.

## 2. The seven

### Wizzle (envy → kindness), sorcerer, joins in Hearthspire (act 1)

| circle | where | what happens | in play |
|---|---|---|---|
| comfort, want | Hearthspire, after the hatching | in Grandma's shadow, too young for an ember; wants to be taken seriously | joins; chat 1 *"You HAD one and you KEPT it?!"* (at the first inn) |
| go | R1, Pebblebrook | glares at every kobold with an ember | chat 2 with Nugget: *who deserves an ember* (Pebblebrook inn) |
| search, find | Emberreach | meets kobolds who had embers and wasted them on smashing; envy stops making sense | chat 3 with Cinder, after the ovens: *"you had one and you used it for THAT?"* |
| pay | the Ordeal | goes on alone; the guards catch him | leaves the roster ("caught by the guards") |
| return | Frostwick, the draft camp | walks out when the fort refuses the draft: "You came back." "Pockets were cold." | rejoins; chat 4: *what the camp was like* |
| changed | the new hatching | his first ember; he cheers everyone else's | quest done at the hatching (+1); chat 5 at the feast |

**His quest** (no battle): in Frostwick, before the rescue, he passes notes out of the camp (found on a fence each night); the
player answers three of them. Talent card unlocked: **Kind spark** (his Spark also heals an ally beside the target 5%).

first point of a foe's resistance).

awake** (Bork cannot be slowed or made to fall asleep, and his Balance counts 5% higher). His class is the generalist monk:
diligence made mechanical (grows by fixing his weakest point).
